Ask an out-of-towner where to see comedy in New York and you'll hear the same two famous names. But the real engine of NYC stand-up is the dozens of rooms running shows every night of the week — clubs where tonight's lineup might include a Netflix name dropping in to work out new material next to the best unknowns in the city.

Here are rooms with packed calendars you can browse right now:

## Dedicated comedy clubs

**[New York Comedy Club — East Village](/venue/9931859d-5b76-4881-97db-5c5cd976cb02)** — One of the city's premier stand-up rooms, with multiple shows nightly featuring comics you'll recognize and ones you're about to.

**[Broadway Comedy Club](/venue/0acbe956-ca20-45fc-b504-5b2cfcf92041)** — A Midtown staple showcasing a rotating mix of established and emerging comedians, with several showtimes a night.

**[Greenwich Village Comedy Club](/venue/a89c1ea7-fce0-474e-bfb5-0b6048f5dd37)** — An intimate Village basement in the neighborhood that basically invented the form's New York era.

**[The Grisly Pear — Midtown](/venue/1c60d37c-a5d9-4118-a1a6-164e4b0ef456)** — A bar-plus-showroom with one of the busiest comedy calendars in Manhattan, heavy on affordable shows.

## The indie side

**[The Tiny Cupboard](/venue/beebb3fd-4a87-47b3-91d8-0d9d081b33ad)** — Brooklyn's hidden gem: an intimate Bushwick venue (yes, really, in an apartment building — with a rooftop) that's become one of the most beloved independent comedy spots in the city.

And that's just the dedicated rooms. All over Brooklyn and Manhattan, bars like **[Freddy's](/venue/b06c778e-f997-42a5-95da-15f5347c8e1e)** and **[Pine Box Rock Shop](/venue/94297e14-e36c-483e-8f35-4df00c12ba2b)** run weekly comedy nights and open mics between their music bookings — the kind of shows that only ever exist as a line on the venue's own calendar.
