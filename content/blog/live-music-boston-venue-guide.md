Boston punches way above its weight for live music — but most of it never shows up on the big ticketing apps. The best nights out here happen in rooms that book their own calendars: folk clubs, jazz bars, black-box stages, breweries with a PA. Here's where to look, with links to each venue's live calendar on Find Local.

## The rock clubs

**[Paradise Rock Club](/venue/f1de7878-2b80-4cf4-9247-c49e2b2e3afd)** — The Comm Ave institution near BU, and still one of the best rooms in the city to catch a touring act right before they get too big for it.

**[The Sinclair](/venue/e0cb6e24-2fed-4e07-9b2b-f0034689a054)** — Harvard Square's mid-size stage, a restaurant-and-venue combo with one of the busiest calendars in Cambridge.

**[Roadrunner](/venue/75680724-5ed4-4967-bf54-76530710ddf7)** — Brighton's newer big room, built for the acts that outgrew the clubs.

**[House of Blues](/venue/25cb224c-7068-4d84-b288-c96e45d35507)** — The Lansdowne Street standby across from Fenway, mixing national tours with theme nights.

## Folk and jazz rooms

**[Club Passim](/venue/3d4b1bd1-7ed9-4fcb-b16c-250d9dece36e)** — The legendary Harvard Square folk club (Joan Baez and Bob Dylan played the room it grew out of). Intimate doesn't begin to cover it.

**[Regattabar](/venue/e1423c55-ad66-4016-ab15-e791ba9c12bc)** — Cambridge's renowned jazz club, back with a steady calendar of live performances.

**[The Lilypad](/venue/b7716a13-e708-4573-8445-ea786a47233e)** — An Inman Square art-space-turned-stage where jazz, experimental, and songwriter nights share the week.

**[Lizard Lounge](/venue/464f124c-5517-47ce-a97e-86fbee76ad04)** — A cozy basement room under Cambridge Common known for up-close sets and long-running residencies.

## Dinner-and-a-show (and a pint)

**[City Winery](/venue/c1306e27-2e7d-48e0-a920-146eb673dcc1)** — A working winery, restaurant, and concert hall in one, with the busiest music calendar in the city — often multiple shows a night across its stages.

**[Somerville Theatre](/venue/e37161c9-1aa9-4f48-857b-ca666d15cccf)** — The historic Davis Square movie palace that moonlights as one of the area's best-sounding live rooms.
